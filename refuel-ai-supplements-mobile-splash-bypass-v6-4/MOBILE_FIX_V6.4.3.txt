Refuel AI Supplements 6.4.3 - normal mobile-browser scroll chaining

The live site was verified to serve 6.4.2. Buttons now work according to
the user, but finger scrolling remains blocked.

The template applies overscroll-behavior-y:contain to mobile-pwa-variant,
including ordinary browser visits. With the document as the root scroller
and an auto-height, overflow:auto body, the body has no vertical scroll
range. Containment prevents gestures from passing to the root scroller.

This update sets overscroll-behavior-y:auto on the unlocked browser body
in the head and footer safeguards. Open modal locks and installed PWA
scroll containers retain their existing rules. No templates, images,
product content, layout or button handlers were changed.

Checks: a new computed-style regression fails on 6.4.2 (contain rather
than auto) and passes after the patch. Existing observer, navigation,
menu, AI, loader, PHP parser and JavaScript syntax tests are also run.
DOM tests are not physical Android touch tests; phone verification is
still required after upload. No changes were deployed to live hosting.

Install: back up the current theme, then WordPress > Appearance > Themes
> Add New > Upload Theme > choose this ZIP > Replace current with uploaded.
Clear Hostinger cache. Close the old phone tab and open the homepage again.
With menus/chat closed, swipe upward over both the offer card and its
surrounding area. Confirm that Top Brands and later sections appear.
Then open and close the menu and verify scrolling still works.
Rollback: upload the previous 6.4.2 ZIP using the same replacement flow.
